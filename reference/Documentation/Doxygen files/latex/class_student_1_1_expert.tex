\subsection{Detailed Description}
The \hyperlink{class_student_1_1_expert}{Expert} class represents an expert in the game. 

The expert can work in various buildings and is needed in nuclear and hydroelectric power plants in order for them to operate. Adding the unit costs money and the expert also has a salary that is payed when the round ends. The unit can conquer new areas too if the tile has no owner
